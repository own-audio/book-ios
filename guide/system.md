# Outside the app

## Lock screen, Control Center and Dynamic Island

While a book plays, the system's Now Playing controls show the cover, the title, the author
and the current chapter, on the lock screen, in Control Center and in the Dynamic Island. They
have:

- play/pause,
- skip back and skip forward by your intervals from [Settings](settings.md#playback),
- a position bar for the whole book, which you can drag.

Headphone and car controls that send "next" and "previous" step to the next or previous
chapter.

## Widgets

The **Continue Listening** widget ("Your last books, a tap away.") comes in two sizes:

- **Small**: the book you were last listening to, with its cover, progress and time left.
- **Medium**: your last three books.

Tap a book in the widget to open the app and play it. To add it, touch and hold the Home Screen,
tap **Edit** and **Add Widget**, and look for own.book.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Siri understands these without any setup (the app is called "own.book" here):

- "Continue listening in own.book", "Continue my book in own.book", "Resume my audiobook in
  own.book": goes on with the book you were listening to.
- "Play *book title* in own.book", "Listen to *book title* in own.book": plays a book from the
  selected server, from where you left off.

The same two actions, **Continue Listening** and **Play a Book**, are in the Shortcuts app.

## AirPlay

The **AirPlay** button in the [player](player.md) sends the book to an AirPlay speaker or TV.

## CarPlay

The app has a CarPlay screen, but it has not been tried on a real CarPlay screen yet, so it
may not work as described.

It lists **Continue Listening**, **Downloads**, **Library**, **Series** and **Authors** for the
selected server (Continue Listening and Downloads come first, because a car often has no
signal). The playing screen has a speed button that steps 1× → 1.25× → … → 2× → 0.75× → 1×,
and a **Chapters** list. With no server added, it says "Add a server on your iPhone".
