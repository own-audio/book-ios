# Privacy

The app collects nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

It talks only to:

- **the servers you add**: your sign-in, requests for your library, the books and covers you
  play, your positions, bookmarks and favorites, and how long you listened (servers use that
  for their listening statistics);
- **Apple or Google**, only if you choose to sign in with them for an own.audio server.

What a server does with that is up to whoever runs it: you, a friend, or a hosting service with
its own policy.

On the iPhone the app keeps your servers and sign-ins (in the Keychain; the password itself is
not kept), your settings and positions, downloaded books, covers, and saved answers from your
servers so it works offline. Deleting the app removes all of it; removing a server removes its
downloads and the library kept for it.

The **Logs** in Settings stay on the iPhone unless you share them yourself.

The full policy: [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/own-audio/book-ios/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
