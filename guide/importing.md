# Adding books (own.audio)

On an own.audio server you can add audiobooks from your iPhone. On Audiobookshelf, books are
added on the server, so the app has no button for it there.

## Importing audio files

In the **Library** tab, tap **+** at the top. (When the server also offers narration, **+**
opens a menu: choose **Import Audio Files…**.) The **Import a Book** screen opens.

1. Tap **Choose Audio Files** and pick files or folders in the Files app (on a Mac, in the
   open panel). A folder adds the
   audio files inside it, in name order. **Add More Files** adds more.
2. The files are listed under **Files, in playing order**, with their lengths and a total. Tap
   **Edit** to drag them into another order or delete one.
3. Under **Book**, the app fills in **Title**, **Author** and the **Cover** from the first
   file's tags. Change them if you like; **Narrator** can be added too. Separate several
   authors or narrators with a semicolon. Tap **Cover** to choose a picture from Photos.
4. **Who sees it**: **Only me** (default) or **The family**.
5. Tap **Add to Library**.

If you are on mobile data and the files are larger than 100 MB, the app asks first: **Upload …
over mobile data?**

The files are copied and uploaded in the background, so you can leave the screen. While they
upload, the Library shows them under **Importing** with a progress bar. A failed upload can be
tried again (**Try Again**) or dropped (**Remove**); swipe one, or touch and hold it
(right-click on a Mac), to **Cancel** it. The book
appears in the library when the upload is done.

Importing needs a connection; the **+** button is hidden while the app is offline.

## Narrating an ebook

Some own.audio servers can read an ebook aloud and turn it into an audiobook. This costs money:
the price is taken from the family's prepaid credit on that server. The option appears only on
a server that offers it; on others, and on Audiobookshelf, there is none.

Where it is offered, **+** in the Library opens a menu with **Narrate an Ebook…**:

1. **Choose an Ebook**: an EPUB, MOBI or plain text file. The file goes to the server, which
   reads it aloud; it is not kept afterwards.
2. Choose the language it is **Written in** and the language to have it **Read in**, a
   **Voice**, and **Files**: **One per chapter** or **One for the whole book**. If the two
   languages differ, the book is translated first.
3. Tap **Get the Price**. The server counts the characters and quotes a price, with
   **Family Credit** showing what the family has. Nothing is charged until you start.
4. Tap **Narrate for …** to start.

The price is taken from the family's credit when the book is done. A narration takes minutes to
hours; the Library shows it under **Narrating** with how far it is ("Narrating · chapter 3 of
12"), and the book appears in the library at the end.
