# Settings

Open Settings with the gear at the top of [Home](home.md). **Done** closes it.

## Servers

Your servers and **Add a Server**. See [Servers](servers.md).

## Downloads and offline

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Downloads** | Opens the list of downloaded books; shows their total size. | — |
| **Offline Mode** | Shows and plays only what is on this iPhone, and asks nothing of the servers. | Off |
| **Download on Wi-Fi Only** | Downloads wait for Wi-Fi. | On |
| **Keep at Most** | A limit for downloads: **No Limit**, 1 GB, 2 GB, 5 GB or 10 GB. Over it, finished books go first, then the ones not played for longest; never the one playing. | No Limit |
| **Whole Library When Offline** | Offline, an own.audio server's whole library is listed from the copy on this iPhone, with books not downloaded greyed out. Only downloaded books play. Shown only when you have an own.audio server. | Off |

More in [Downloads and offline](downloads.md).

## Playback

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Skip Back** | How far the skip back button goes: 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 s. Also on the lock screen. | 15 s |
| **Skip Forward** | How far the skip forward button goes, same choices. | 30 s |
| **Speed for New Books** | The speed a book starts at the first time: 0.75× to 2×. After that each book keeps its own speed. | 1× |
| **Smart Rewind** | Goes back a few seconds when you resume after a pause, more after a longer one. See [the player](player.md#smart-rewind). | On |

## Streaming

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **On Mobile Data** | **Original** streams the original files. **Saver** asks the server for a smaller stream (64 kbps) where it makes one. **Downloaded Only** plays nothing that is not on this iPhone. | Original |
| **Keep Ahead** | While a book streams, how many minutes ahead are kept on the iPhone, so a gap in the signal goes unheard: 5, 10 or 15 min. With Low Data Mode, 5 minutes. Saver streams are not kept. | 10 min |

## Accent Colour

A row of colours for buttons, links and what's playing: **App Colour** (the app's brown, the
default), **Blue**, **Indigo**, **Purple**, **Pink**, **Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**, **Green**,
**Teal** and **Graphite**. The app icon and the widgets keep the app's colour.

The app follows your iPhone's light or dark appearance.

## About

- **Version**: the app's version and build number.
- **Source Code and Licence**: the app's code on GitHub.
- **Privacy**: the privacy policy.
- **Logs**: what the app wrote to its log since it started: failed requests, playback trouble,
  sync. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it. **Share Log** (top right) makes a text file
  to attach to a bug report. Addresses are logged without their sign-in tokens.
