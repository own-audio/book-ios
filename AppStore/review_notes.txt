No account is needed to try the app: on the welcome screen, "Try the Demo Library" signs in to the public own.audio demo server (demopi.own.audio) by itself. It holds public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox; open Library and play one. Chapters, speed, the sleep timer and bookmarks are in the player.

The app plays audiobooks from servers the listener runs or rents: own.audio and Audiobookshelf. A reviewer would need their own Audiobookshelf server for that; everything in the app works the same way on both.

Some parts appear only when the server offers them: the family and its shared books, listening statistics, adding books from the Files app, and sign-in with Apple or Google. The app sells nothing and links to no purchase.

Deleting an account: Settings → the server → Delete Account… The demo's shared account is protected by the server, so deleting it is refused with an explanation; on any other own.audio account it deletes the account and everything it added.

The app is free and open source (MPL-2.0): https://github.com/own-audio/book-ios
