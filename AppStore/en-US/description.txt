A native player for the audiobooks on your own server: Audiobookshelf, and own.audio.

Made for listening
• Speed from 0.5× to 3× with the voice's pitch kept, remembered per book
• Chapters: from the server, or read from the files themselves when the server has none
• Skip back and forward by the seconds you choose; smart rewind after a pause
• Sleep timer: minutes, the end of the chapter, or the end of the book
• Bookmarks with a label, kept on your server
• Lock screen and Control Center with skip buttons and chapter steps

Your whole library
• Continue Listening and Recently Added, as your server arranges them
• Series, authors, narrators and collections, where your server has them
• Sort and filter: not started, in progress, finished
• Search by title, author, narrator and series

Your place, everywhere
• One position per book, saved on this iPhone first and then on your server, so a lost signal never loses your place
• Positions and bookmarks follow you between devices and apps that use the same server

At home and away
• Several servers at once
• A server's home and outside addresses: the app uses whichever answers, and only if it is the same server
• On mobile data: the original, a smaller stream, or nothing but what is on the iPhone
• iPhone and iPad, in English and Czech

With an own.audio server
• A library shared with the family, where you choose what is shared
• The library on the iPhone catches up within seconds of a change on the server

Private by design: the app talks only to the servers you add. No account with us, no analytics, no ads. Both kinds of server hand the app a sign-in token; the password is not kept.

Free and open source under the MPL-2.0. Made by own.audio.
