# Privacy policy — own.audio Books

own.audio Books is a player for audiobook servers you choose: Audiobookshelf, and own.audio.
This page says what the app keeps and what it sends. Last updated 15 October 2026.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
server of ours that the app reports to. We, the makers of the app, receive no data from it.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- **To the servers you add**, and only to them: your sign-in, requests for your library, the
  books and covers you play, where you are in each book, your bookmarks and favourites, and
  how long you listened (servers use that for their listening statistics). What a server does
  with them is up to whoever runs it — you, a friend, or a hosting service with its own policy
  (for own.audio's hosted service: https://www.own.audio/privacy/).
- **To Google**, only if you choose "Sign in with Google" for an own.audio server: the sign-in
  happens on Google's page, and the app passes the result to your server.
- **To Apple**, only if you choose "Sign in with Apple", in the same way. Apple may also send
  crash reports to us if you agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone's
  settings; they contain no library or account data.

## What stays on your iPhone

- The servers you added and how to sign in to them, in the iPhone's Keychain. Both kinds of
  server hand the app a sign-in token; the password is not kept.
- Your settings, your position in each book, downloaded books, covers and saved answers from
  your servers (so the app works offline). Deleting the app removes all of it; removing a
  server removes its library kept on the phone.

## Children

The app has no age-specific features and collects nothing from anyone.
